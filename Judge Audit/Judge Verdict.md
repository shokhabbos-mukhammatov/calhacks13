# Judge verdict

The shortlist and the idea pool, stress-tested from the judges’ side of the table. Who the judges are and what they ask: [[Judge Panel]].

## Bottom line
1. **[[Fire Drill]] stays #1.** It has the widest panel appeal. Fix: a one-line answer to existing voice-agent testing platforms, and a demo that survives a loud hall. → [[Audit - Fire Drill]]
2. **[[Netting House]] has the highest ceiling** with [[Hyphenate]], [[Fetch.ai]] and quant judges. Fix: credit Cycles and lead with one person’s story. → [[Audit - Netting House]]
3. **[[Vault Heist]] is the highest-variance pick**: the best crowd demo and best [[SpacetimeDB]] / [[Supercell]] fit, but it needs real players and careful balancing. → [[Audit - Vault Heist]]
4. **[[Two-Face]] is the best web-data track fit with the biggest “looks staged” risk.** Fix: real-site evidence, or lead with the firewall variant. → [[Audit - Two-Face]]

## Pool ideas that move
**Down**
- [[Stump Market]] is essentially [Humanity’s Last Exam](https://scale.com/blog/humanitys-last-exam), which [[Scale]] (a sponsor) ran with CAIS: a $500K prize pool for questions that stump frontier models. A Scale judge would say “we did this.”
- [[AgentFICO]] faces funded startups: AgentScore (“credit bureau for the AI economy”, watching x402 payments), Vouched (Know Your Agent, $17M Series A) and Alien. Its novelty is gone.
- [[Jailbreak Booth]] is Gandalf with a kiosk.

**Up** (judgment calls, not researched as deeply)
- [[Backlog Tower Defense]]: a judge files a bug from their phone and watches it die when a Droid’s fix merges. It’s participatory and fun, and covers SpacetimeDB, Atlassian and Factory. Risk: PR turnaround time. Seed small bugs.
- [[Close Raid]] puts two Tier 1 sponsors ([[Hyphenate]] and [[SpacetimeDB]]) plus Supercell in one game. It’s a lighter alternative if Netting House feels too heavy.
- [[Flytrap]]: security judges love honeypots, and a live attacker agent walking into a decoy is easy to show.

## Mistakes that would cost us with any idea
1. **Logo soup.** Our idea notes list 6–8 sponsors each. Enter 3 primary tracks plus Anthropic; mention others only if they do real work.
2. **Thin commit history.** Commit from hour one so the git log tells the story.
3. **No network plan.** Bring a hotspot, run agents locally where possible, record the demo video by hour 31.
4. **Unprepared Q&A.** Rehearse the killer questions in each audit note. Assign each teammate the questions they answer.
5. **Missing ethics framing** for the security ideas: own agents only, synthetic voices, PG content, polite crawling.
6. **No numbers.** Each demo should end on one table (breach rates, money moved, precision/recall). Numbers are what technical and quant judges remember.
