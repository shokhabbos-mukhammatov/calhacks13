# Recent winners (2026)

Scraped from Devpost on Oct 20–21: every project page, winners vs non-winners.

## UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026 (same organizers as Cal Hacks), 399 projects, 55 winners
- Grand prizes: **Theracat** (wristband + plush cat that catches anxiety and interrupts it), **Lucid Voice** (a few taps become a sentence in your own voice), **Paper Cuts** (draw a doodle, it becomes a playable game object; also Best UI/UX), **IronBook** (training for manufacturing workers).
- Finalists: Inspector (agents that QA your app by using it, also Hacker's Choice), FirstResponder-Relay (multilingual 911 overflow triage during wildfires), Sentinel, StudForge, Percept, SnapCycle.
- Win rate by team size: solo 7%, 2 → 11%, 3 → 17%, 4 → 21%. With a video 15% vs 9% without. Write-up length didn't matter.
- Same idea, different result: **Aegis** (live 911 console) won vs **SIREN** (upload a recording, get a report) lost. **FirstResponder-Relay** (local Bay Area fire maps, judges call in from their phones) was a finalist vs **RELAY** (same tech, Houston story) lost. **Captain Ddoski** (benchmarked with vs without the sponsor) won vs **Foreman** (invoice agents, no numbers) lost.

## Patterns
- **One sentence, one moment.** Every grand prize can be explained in a line and shown in one live moment.
- **Judges take part:** they call in, play it, draw on it, or try to break it.
- **Proof beats claims:** a benchmark or before/after number.
- **Agents checking agents** is trending: LA Hacks 1st and 3rd.
- **Physical or playable** things keep reaching the finals.
- **Local and specific** beats generic: real places, real stats, real interviews.
