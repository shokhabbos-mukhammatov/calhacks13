# Prizes and rules (posted on Devpost, checked Oct 21)

Source: https://cal-hacks-13-0.devpost.com · **66 prizes, $126,988 listed as cash** (Creao counts credits as cash, see below).

## Rules that change our plan
- **Teams of 2 to 4** (Devpost setting), yet there is also a **Best Solo Hack** prize. Ask organizers before going solo.
- Be at your table by **Sun Oct 25, 8 AM**. Submit by **9 AM** with your teammates. Edits allowed until **10 AM**, except the table number.
- Submission needs an image, the **GitHub repo**, the **table number**, and a **demo video saved locally**.
- **All work must be done during the hackathon.**
- Judging: **3-minute pitch + 2 minutes of questions**.

## Judging criteria (Cal Hacks prizes)
- **Application**: realistic real-world use; who it helps and why they'd use it.
- **Intentionality**: intuitive experience, every design choice has a reason.
- **Functionality**: smooth live demo, no major bugs, plus a clear video for sponsors judging asynchronously.
- **Creativity**: original framing, unexpected combinations.
- **Technical complexity**: sound architecture, depth beyond a basic implementation.
